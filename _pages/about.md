I am an undergraduate student at the University of Michigan (B.S. expected May 2027), majoring in Computer Science, Data Science, Cognitive Science, and BCN (Biopsychology, Cognition, and Neuroscience). I am working in the [Multisensory Perception Lab](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/brang-lab/), advised by Dr. David Brang.

My research investigates how visual speech cues influence auditory speech processing and how audiovisual integration supports speech comprehension. In my current work, I use intracranial electroencephalography (iEEG) to investigate how visual information shapes the neural encoding of speech and language.

More broadly, I am interested in how the brain dynamically represents speech signals across multiple levels of processing, from acoustic features to sentence-level meaning. I am particularly interested in how the brain integrates acoustic, linguistic, and visual information across time to support predictive and hierarchical language processing.

My name is pronounced *RAY-shee Pyao* (朴睿熙).
